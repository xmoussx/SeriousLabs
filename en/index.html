The problem

The money does not leak where you are looking.

In a firm of under twenty people, nobody has time to measure where the hours go. They do not go into the work — they go around the work. Into emails rewritten three times, proposals restarted from scratch, documents nobody can find, meeting notes pushed to Saturday.

Those hours are never billed. They are paid for all the same.

Every week

Rewriting what already exists

The proposal started from a blank page, when forty versions of it already exist. The same follow-up email, reworded for the hundredth time.

Every week

Searching instead of finding

Locating the right clause, the right rate, the right client file. The information is already in the building — it is simply unfindable at the moment you need it.

Every week

Pushing admin to the evening

Follow-ups, meeting notes, tracking. What is never urgent becomes Sunday work, then a backlog, then a hole in your cash flow.

Every week

Keeping the client waiting

A quote that goes out on day five instead of day one loses one deal in five. The cost never shows up in the accounts — it shows up in the order book.

The engagement

One day at your offices, and it is in place.

No platform to rent, no six-month programme, no consultant turning up every Tuesday. We come, we train, we install, we leave. The tools stay with you and belong to you.

Before · 45 minutes

We look at your week

A call to understand what is eating your hours. We leave with your real working documents, so the day is built on your cases rather than generic examples.

The day · morning

We train the whole team

Everyone in the same room, working on their own files. By lunch, each person has already produced something usable. No technical prerequisites.

The day · afternoon

We install it properly

Tools configured on your machines, with your templates, your vocabulary, your files. Nothing to reinstall tomorrow, nothing that depends on us being there.

After · 30 days

We check that it held

A review at one month. Whatever is not being used gets fixed or removed: a tool nobody opens is a cost, not an investment.

Nothing leaves your firm without someone on your team having written it, read it and stood behind it. That is the one rule we do not negotiate.
