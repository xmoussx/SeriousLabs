Where your hours go.

In a firm of under twenty people, nobody has the time to measure where the hours go. They go into everything that sits around the work: emails rewritten three times, quotes started again from scratch because nobody can lay hands on the last one, write-ups pushed to Saturday.

These hours never appear on an invoice. Here are the jobs we take on. In every case, your team reads and signs off whatever goes out. The times shown are weekly savings per person involved, and they are deliberately cautious.

How the day goes.

There is no platform to rent and no subscription ticking away in the background. We come for the day, and once more at thirty days. The tools are installed on your own machines and you own them outright.

Before · 45 minutes

We look at your week

A call to understand what is taking your time. We leave with your real working documents and build the day around them.

The day · morning

Everyone learns on their own files

Everyone in the same room, with the week's live files. We do not leave the morning until each person has set up at least one use that genuinely helps them, and no technical background is required.

The day · afternoon

The tools land on your machines

The tools are set up with your own document templates and connected to your files. None of them sends anything on its own. Nothing to reinstall the next morning.

After · 30 days

We come back a month later

A review one month on. If a tool is not being used, we sort it out on the spot, and we take it away if that does not work.

No document leaves your office unless someone on your team has written it, read it and stood behind it.

We do not negotiate it, even on the days when it costs time. In practice, someone at your firm reads every message before it goes out, including three-line chasers.
